## Hugo body

Goal of the project was to create a trading bot which will trade cryptocurrencies on few exchanges like Binance etc.

#### Project Details

After analyzing exchange of interest it was clear that particular exchange was lagging behind due to different time zone and relative small size of it. So, by identifying general trends in markets for specific cryptocurrency like BTC we can capitalize on that.

In addition, I have developed and deployed a trading bot which was listening to signals from Tradingview custom strategy.

Important question is whether we made money. The answer is it depends.. We made some money but due to high trading fees and changing environment algorithm should be constantly monitored and tweaked.
