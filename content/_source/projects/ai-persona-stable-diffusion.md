## Hugo body

Project goals were to develop AI model like Aitana Lopez (@fit_aitana)

#### Project Details

AI generated content gained immense popularity. Not just textual data but also images and videos generated by AI became high in demand.

I have utilized the Stable Diffusion Automatic1111 tool to generate realistic images. This tool, known for its high-quality output, will be run on a local machine with a powerful NVIDIA 2080 Ti GPU to ensure efficient processing and quick turnaround times.

The process included

1. Model Setup: Install and configure the Stable Diffusion Automatic1111 tool on the local machine.

2. Data Preparation: Collect and preprocess data to fine-tune the model for generating images aligned with the desired aesthetic.

3. Model Training: Fine-tune the Stable Diffusion model on the prepared dataset, leveraging the computational power of the 2080 Ti GPU.

4. Content Generation: Generate daily content to maintain engagement and grow the follower base. This involves creating images that are both realistic and captivating.
