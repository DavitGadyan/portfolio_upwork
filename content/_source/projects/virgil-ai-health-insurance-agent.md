## Hugo body

Project goals were to automate onaboarding and assist US users to find best insurance plans without any hustle using AI Agentic system with support of custom tools, MCPs.

### Project Details

🚀 Virgil HealthCare AI was a developed to assist with user healtcare plan selection, a cumbersome task for many US citizens. One Health was broker agent collaborating with our team on this project. There were many challenging tasks that were automated like medication price efficient lookup to detect whether applicant was covered by different health care plans, underwriting done in minutes instead of days.

All AI and data was stored and trasnfered securely guided by HIPPA regualtions and best security practices.

Huge potenrial user base and ease of use for this application compared to market standards made it really viable option for clients to use.

### 🧠 Technologies Used

- **Langchain** – Langchain Framework
- **Fast API** – AI Backend Rest Framework
- **OpenAI GPT-o4** – Backbone Reasoning model
- **Docker** – Virtualiztion
- **Kubernetess** – Scaling
- **AWS** – SAAS, PAAS, IAAS platform
