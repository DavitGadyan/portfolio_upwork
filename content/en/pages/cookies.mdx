This site uses as few cookies as possible. Nothing that tracks you is set until you choose **Accept all cookies** in the banner, and you can change your mind at any time with the **Change cookie preferences** button on this page or the **Manage cookies** link in the footer.

## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for a site to work; others are used to measure how the site is used. This site also uses browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage), which works like a cookie but never leaves your device.

## Cookies and storage used on this site

### Essential (always on)

These are needed to remember your choices. They are not used for tracking and do not require consent.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `dg_consent` | cookie + localStorage | Remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics cookies, and the version of this policy you answered. | 12 months |
| `dg_lang` | localStorage | Remembers the language you picked with the EN / ES switch. | Until cleared |

### Analytics (only after "Accept all cookies")

Used to understand which pages are read and which links and downloads are used. The data is aggregated; I do not try to identify individual visitors.

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga` | Google Analytics 4 | Distinguishes one browser from another with a random id. | 2 years |
| `_ga_*` | Google Analytics 4 | Keeps session state for the same property. | 2 years |

Google Consent Mode v2 is used: until you accept, no Google script is loaded and nothing is sent to Google. If you decline, or later withdraw consent, the analytics cookies are deleted and the script is not loaded again.

### Third-party content loaded on request

Embedded videos load content from other companies **only when you click them**. Those companies may then set their own cookies under their own policies.

| Feature | Provider | When it loads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Embedded videos | YouTube (privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` domain) and Loom | When you press play on a video. |

## How to change your choice

- Press **Change cookie preferences** on this page or **Manage cookies** in the footer to reopen the banner.
- You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Most browsers let you block cookies entirely; the essential parts of this site keep working if you do.
