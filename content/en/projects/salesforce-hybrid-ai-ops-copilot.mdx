## The problem

Teams hiring for Salesforce and AI work keep describing the same problems. Legal worries that customer data will leak to an outside model. Finance worries about model bills and being locked into one vendor. Admins worry that AI-written Apex and Flows look fine and then break in production. Sales has thousands of old leads nobody works, and every sandbox refresh or org migration eats days of admin time.

This project answers each of those with something working, not slides.

## What I built

![AI router: a patient-intake record is routed to the private LLM because it contains medical data, and the decision trace shows it stayed inside the network](/images/portfolio/sf-ops-private-llm.jpg)

**A router that decides where data may go.** Every request is checked for sensitive fields. Medical and card data goes only to a model inside the client's network. The decision trace shows which policy matched and why.

![Masked prompt: names, e-mails and phones replaced with tokens before Claude sees the record, then restored in the answer](/images/portfolio/sf-ops-masked-prompt.jpg)

**Masking before anything leaves.** Names, e-mails and phone numbers are replaced with tokens before Claude or ChatGPT see the record, then put back in the answer. Salesforce stays the system of record and the best model still does the job.

![Model comparison: the same Salesforce opportunity assessed by Claude Opus, Claude Haiku and a private model, with latency and cost per answer](/images/portfolio/sf-ops-benchmark.jpg)

**Cost decided on data, not a vendor pitch.** A compare view runs the same record across every connected model, so the routing choice rests on real cost and quality. Adding or swapping a provider is a setting, not a rebuild.

![Apex review: findings on a payment class, then the corrected Queueable code shown as a diff](/images/portfolio/sf-ops-code-review.jpg)

**Apex and Flow review that fixes the code.** Fixed rules catch queries inside loops, missing sharing and field security, hard-coded IDs and keys. Claude then confirms each finding, adds the ones rules cannot see, and returns corrected code with a bulk test class.

![Refresh and migrate: record reconciliation between source and target, and Claude's architect review with a runbook](/images/portfolio/sf-ops-refresh-review.jpg)

**Sandbox refreshes and migrations, planned.** After a refresh someone has to mask personal data, point integrations at test endpoints, stop scheduled jobs and reload test data, usually from memory. The planner builds that checklist from the org's own metadata, orders the data seeding so lookups resolve, maps fields for an org-to-org move, reconciles record counts, and has Claude review the plan and write the runbook.

**People stay in control.** The revenue agent scores and tiers each lead, picks a next best action and drafts a short, specific e-mail. Any draft that cites something not in the CRM goes back for rewrite, and nothing is sent or updated until a person approves it. Every AI decision is logged.
